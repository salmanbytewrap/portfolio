01 — About

Nine years of turning fragile systems into reliable ones

I've spent my career on the parts of the stack that aren't optional: payment processing, database migrations, and the APIs everything else depends on. Most of my best work has started with a system that was already fragile — an RxJS fintech backend, undocumented insurer APIs, a Firebase datastore that needed to move without anyone noticing — and ended with something that scales.

Recently that's extended into AI-powered features in production: an LLM-based churn-detection module at Daxko's SugarWOD platform that improved member retention by 40%, built with the same discipline I'd apply to a payments system — tested, monitored, and shipped incrementally.

I've operated as an individual contributor, a Tech Lead directing a 12-engineer team, and an acting Scrum Lead — I'm equally comfortable in the code and in the room where the architecture gets decided.
